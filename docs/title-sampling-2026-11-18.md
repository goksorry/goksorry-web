# Title sentiment samples — 2026-11-18

The worker classifies titles only in batches of up to 40, every ten minutes. Up to 120 unique eligible titles are selected per run, reduced according to measured cost and remaining daily/call allowance. The local daily estimate remains $3. Half the selected slots provide site coverage; the remainder follows eligible candidate volume. This is a sample of collected public lists, not exhaustive community coverage.

Classic and world index displays show six-hour total and directional (bullish + bearish) counts. A community needs 30 directional classifications before its numeric index, ratio gauge and sentiment portrait are displayed. The overall average waits until every directional contributor meets that guard. Neutral-only or empty sources do not cast a neutral vote. Source links remain accessible for small samples.

The threshold is an operational display guard, not a statistical confidence threshold. Larger samples can still be biased by collection coverage, repeated authors, title ambiguity and classification errors. Internal confidence is the model's interpretation confidence, not a margin of error. The index formula, stored numeric buckets and API contracts remain unchanged.

Validation covers empty/29/30-directional cases, high neutral counts, mixed community sizes, preserved numeric aggregates, classic and mobile-world display, and title-link access. Market indicators, account functions and collection permissions are unchanged.
